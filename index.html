For sellers

Pro venditoribus

If you’re a monastery, convent, parish or Catholic small business, you can have your own shop on Merchants Guild. You don’t need a website or any technical know-how.

  1. I

    Get in touch

    Fill in our short form to tell us who you are and what you make. It takes about five minutes.

  2. II

    We set up your shop

    Send us photos and prices and we’ll put your listings together with you.

  3. III

    Start selling

    Orders come to you, you post them out, and you get paid.

Why we’re doing this

Cur hoc facimus

As Catholics, whenever we bought gifts, we wanted the money to support the Church in some way: a monastery, a parish, or simply a Catholic family business. But they were hard to find. The things they make are spread across abbey gift shops, convent gates and small websites, and there was nowhere to see them all in one place.

So we’re building one. The Guild brings Catholic makers together on one page, so you can buy for birthdays, feast days, Christmas or any special day, knowing your money is helping a Catholic community or family business.
