For sellers

If you’re a monastery, convent, parish or Catholic small business, you can have your own shop on Merchants Guild. You don’t need a website or any technical know-how.

    Get in touch

  2. II

    We set up your shop

    Send us photos and prices and we’ll put your listings together with you.

  3. III

    Start selling

    Orders come to you, you post them out, and you get paid.

Why we’re doing this

Cur hoc facimus

We’re a Catholic couple, and every Christmas we’d try to buy gifts that supported the Church in some way. It was harder than it should be. The monasteries making cheese and soap were hard to find, and most of them had no way to sell online.

So we’re building one. We want Catholics to be able to buy for birthdays, feast days and Christmas knowing the money is helping a religious community or a Catholic family business.
